Image Vault

A shared gallery: everything uploaded here is visible to everyone. You can delete only the images you added.

Setup required Open index.html and paste your Supabase project URL and public key at the top of the script, then reload.

Accepted: PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP, AVIF (max 5 MB) and MP3, MP4 (max 25 MB). Uploads are public.

Use a direct link that ends in .gif, .png, .jpg, .webp, .mp4, .webm or .mp3. GIF links copied from Discord (.mp4) work too and play on a loop. Temporary Discord attachment links expire, so for those, upload the file instead.
